### Why the second box, not the third

The literal STOP condition — no reliable availability marker — is met. The owner nevertheless chose this spreadsheet as the only source on 2026-09-02, and spec §3.1 was amended in commit 40a7fa2 to absorb the missing marker: a null `availability_status` can never exclude a listing, and availability lives only in the in-memory overlay, re-checked at booking confirmation. Curation therefore drops only records explicitly marked unavailable (none in this source) and keeps null as *not stated*. Given that amendment, STOP would contradict a decision already taken; PROCEED is impossible because eight fields are never published. This box is the honest remainder.

### What this dataset is, in one paragraph

3,216 listings over 566 localities: 217 localities sit at the 10-per-locality ceiling and 349 below it (minimum 1). Every record carries coordinates, rent, deposit, BHK, bedrooms, bathrooms, balconies, property type, furnishing, floor area, a yes/no parking flag and a builder name; total floors is stated for apartments only. Dedupe merged 93 records into 86 winners (same locality, within 50 m, same rent and BHK). Per `data/SOURCE_NOTES.md`, coordinates and locality names are real but rent, deposit and every other descriptive column are randomly generated placeholders — the grounding discipline is testable on them; the prices are not market facts and the demo must not present them as such.

### Two costs the plan did not anticipate (for the owner, before Phase 1)

1. **566 localities.** Task 1.1 asks for 1–3 hand-picked guide pages per locality; Task 1.3 runs 8 OpenStreetMap queries per listing (3,216 × 8 = 25,728 calls). Both were sized for a source with tens of localities, not hundreds. The reverse-geocoded locality names also include building and estate names (Ajmera Infinity Apartments, Concorde Manhattans Apartments, AB Gardenia, BDA Chandragiri Apartments and others) alongside true neighbourhoods, so some of the 566 are not localities in the spec's sense and would have no guide document to cite. Restricting the bundle to a named locality set, or to localities with at least N rows, is a spec §1 decision the owner can still take before Task 1.1; the curation code needs no change to honour it.
2. **No availability marker at all.** The stale-listing behaviour (spec §3.1, §6.43) can only be demonstrated through the operator toggle, never from the data.

Cost of the import: 9,180 rows, under 1 minute (import 2 s, dedupe and curation 12 s, manifest 1 s).
