# Google Stitch prompt — Voice Property Scout UI

Paste everything below the line into Google Stitch as one prompt.

---

Design a dark-themed desktop web app called **Nakshatra — Voice Property Scout**, a voice-first assistant that helps a renter find a flat in Bengaluru, explains each pick with sources, and books a site visit. Simple, calm and beautiful. Generous whitespace, one accent colour, no clutter. Desktop only (min width 1280 px); no mobile layouts, no login, no navigation bar, no listing photos, no maps.

**Visual language.** Background near-black with a faint navy tint (#0B0D14). Surfaces one step lighter (#12141D) with a 1 px border (#1F2230), 16 px radius, no drop shadows. Text: primary #ECEEF5, secondary #8B90A5, muted #5C607A. One accent: indigo-periwinkle (#6D6BFF to #A9B4FF). Three semantic colours used sparingly: green #34C77B (good / "by route"), amber #E8A33D (a result with nothing matched), red #F0555C (an error, a capability is down). Font: Geist or Inter, tight letter-spacing on headings, tabular numbers for money.

**The microphone is the hero.** Screen 1 (welcome, before any conversation): the page is mostly empty. Centred: a large 112 px circular mic button with an indigo gradient fill and a soft outer glow, a white mic glyph, and beneath it the label "Tap to talk to Nakshatra". Above it, small and quiet: "Nakshatra · your Bengaluru property scout". Below it, three faint example chips: "2 BHK in Koramangala under ₹35,000", "near a metro, semi-furnished", "why this one?". A tiny status dot top-right reads "Connected" (green) / "Connecting…" (amber). Nothing else on the screen.

**During the conversation the mic button becomes an orb.** From the click until the user ends the session, the centre of the screen shows a 220 px sphere: deep indigo at the top blending to pale periwinkle and near-white at the bottom, with slow-drifting, semi-transparent white cloud wisps swirling inside it, sitting on the dark background with a soft indigo glow around it (think a cloudy blue planet, soft-edged, no hard outline). Show it in three states: **Listening** (slow breathing scale 1.00 to 1.04, wisps drifting gently, caption "Listening…"), **Thinking** (orb dims slightly, a faint ring pulses outward, caption "Thinking…"), **Speaking** (wisps swirl faster and brighter, glow strengthens, caption "Nakshatra is speaking"). Under the orb: a single live transcript line in secondary text showing the renter's words as they are recognised, then, once final, Nakshatra's reply in primary text (max three sentences). Under that, a small ghost button "End conversation". Never render a waveform or bars; the orb is the only voice visual.

**Screen 2 (live conversation with results).** Layout: the orb column on the left (about 38 % width, orb centred vertically, transcript and reply beneath it), and a results column on the right. At the top of the results column, a row of small readback chips showing what Nakshatra understood: "2 BHK", "Koramangala, HSR Layout", "under ₹35,000", "move in Oct 2026", each with a subtle tick when confirmed. Below, the shortlist grouped by locality: a group heading "Koramangala · 3", then listing cards in a single column. Each card shows, in this order: locality as the card title, society name beneath it, then a compact grid of Rent, Deposit, Maintenance, BHK, Size (sq ft, with "carpet" or "built-up" after the number), Floor, Parking, Furnishing, Available from, and an amenities row of small tags. Any missing value is the literal text "not stated" in muted colour, never blank, never zero. Each card ends with a commute row: "Metro · 1.1 km" with a pill badge that is either **"by route"** (solid green outline pill) or **"straight-line"** (dashed amber outline pill, italic). Include one optional second row "Work · 6 km · straight-line". Card rank number top-right. A small "Why this one?" ghost button on each card. Below the groups, a soft notice: "3 more where the deposit is not stated — want to see them?". Use rupees with Indian grouping (₹35,000; ₹1,20,000).

**Screen 3 (explanation).** Same layout; the selected card is highlighted with an accent left border, and a panel opens beside it titled "Why this one". First line is bold (the fact-led opener, e.g. "₹32,000 for a 2 BHK, 1.1 km from Koramangala metro by route."). Then three to four short claim sentences, each followed by a tiny numbered citation chip. Then a "Gaps" list in muted text ("I don't have a deposit figure for this listing"). Then a "Sources" list: every entry has a label such as "[Wikipedia — Koramangala]" or "[OSM routing — precomputed 2026-09-01]", linked where a URL exists, never a bare "[OSM]". If neighbourhood data is thin, show a quiet tag "Limited neighbourhood data".

**Screen 4 (booking).** A right-side panel titled "Book a visit". Three slot options as selectable rows ("Tue 8 Sep · 4:00–5:00 pm IST"). An email field with the address echoed letter by letter beneath it in mono spacing ("k · a · r · t · h · i · k · @ …"). A confirmed state: the 6-character visit code very large in a mono font (e.g. "K7M4PX"), the listing and slot, "PDF sent to your email" with a tick, a small note "Calendar still syncing" when applicable, and two ghost buttons "Reschedule" and "Cancel". Below the panel, a compact "Have a code?" entry: one input plus "Look up". Owner contact shown as "999999999 · demo placeholder".

**Screen 5 (states sheet).** Show these as separate components on one artboard, each clearly different from the others:
- Question prompt: a soft accent-bordered bubble, "Which do you mean by 'thirty five' — ₹35,000 a month?" with option chips.
- Empty result (amber left border, amber-tinted surface): "Nothing under ₹25,000 in Koramangala." Lists the binding requirement, two suggestions ("try ₹30,000", "add HSR Layout"), and the sentence "I won't relax anything myself."
- Failure banner (red left border, red-tinted surface, must look nothing like the empty result): "Explanation is unavailable right now." with a "Retry" button. The capability name is always shown (speech recognition / understanding / explanation / voice output / calendar / email).
- Degraded notice: a thin amber line "Shortlist shown; explanation withheld — the explanation service did not answer."
- "Can't reach the service" full-width panel with a Retry button, and "This page is out of date with the service — reload" panel.
- Microphone denied: recovery text for Chrome, Firefox and Safari, plus a text input fallback "Type instead".
- "Enable voice" button (audio blocked by the browser) and a "Voice output is temporarily unavailable" toast.
- Reload notice: "Your conversation was not saved; a confirmed booking still works with its code."

Keep every screen restrained: at most one accent gradient (the orb / mic), everything else flat. Sample data must be Bengaluru localities (Koramangala, HSR Layout, Indiranagar, Whitefield), Indian rupees and IST times.
